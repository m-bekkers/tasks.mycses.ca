CSES Affiliation Resources

Start here to understand the affiliation process and prepare questions for CSES.

Current eligibility criteria, required documents, and formal policies are not published on this page. Request the latest CSES materials before treating any checklist as official.

Ask CSES about affiliation →

01

Understand the process

Contact CSES to confirm the current steps, eligibility requirements, timelines, and who is responsible for reviewing an affiliation request. Requirements may change, so use the current guidance supplied by CSES.

02

Prepare your questions

It may help to have your group's name, purpose, primary contact, and an overview of your current membership ready when you ask about affiliation. These are preparation suggestions, not a statement of required application documents.

03

Get the current policies

Ask CSES for the latest written affiliation policies and procedures, including any ongoing responsibilities, review dates, and required approvals that apply to your group. Confirm which version is authoritative before relying on older copies.

To ask for the current affiliation package, open the request form, choose “Something else,” and describe your affiliation question.

01

What is CSES Affiliation?

CSES affiliation is the process by which an undergraduate club or organization can seek recognition within CSES and gain access to its Affiliate benefits. Some such benefits include insurance coverage, Canva Pro licenses, and website hosting.

02

When can I affiliate?

Once affiliation is open for the year, it will remain open until the end of the year. However, student groups are encouraged to apply prior to the start of the academic year in September in order to ensure that their application can be processed and any necessary corrections are made in a timely manner.

03

How do I affiliate?

Each student group will have to abide by CSES’s policies and procedures which govern affiliates. As such, a prospective affiliate’s constitution must be in conformity with said policies and procedures.

Once you are ready to apply, the following will need to be submitted:

  • The name of your organization
  • A short paragraph explaining the purpose of your organization
  • Your approximate year of establishment (if known)
  • Any other affiliations you may have
  • Name and email address of a point of contact
  • Name and email address of the president
  • Name and email address of the VPF or equivalent
  • Your desired affiliation class
  • Name and email of faculty advisor (if applicable)
  • A PDF copy of your most recent constitution
  • A copy of your most recent election results
  • Valid submissions must clearly show vote counts for each candidate, the presence of a “no confidence” option, and not divulge the names of the voters.
  • A list of your organization’s membership, including each member’s cmail, student number, and program
  • Your office location (if applicable)
  • A copy of your logo (if applicable)
  • A link to your Instagram page (if applicable)
  • A link to your Facebook page (if applicable)
  • A link to your website (if applicable)
  • A link to your Linktree (if applicable)
Upon submitting your application, please allow for 1-2 weeks for processing, and up to 3 weeks during peak season (mid-to-end August)

04

Affiliation Classes

Organizations seeking affiliation under CSES fall into one of 5 classes of affiliate:

  • Engineering Club
  • Engineering Academic Society
  • Engineering Design Team
  • Non-Engineering Affiliate
  • Special

Any class of affiliation containing “Engineering” in the name must have a membership composed of more than 50% engineering students and must in some way have a mandate which is related to the field of engineering or design. The full list of requirements for each class of affiliation can be found in the CSES Affiliate Policy.

To ask for the current affiliation package, open the request form, choose “Something else,” and describe your affiliation question.
